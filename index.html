How is the score calculated?
Required phrase checks contribute up to 75 points. A recognised source identifier with no unknown identifiers adds 25 points. Each flagged claim or unknown source identifier subtracts 25 points, with a floor of zero. An empty answer always scores zero.

            
A passed check is a starting point. This demo checks selected patterns and source IDs. It does not understand meaning, verify every claim, or establish safety. Human review is still needed.
